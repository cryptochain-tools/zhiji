# Code of Conduct

Zhiji contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and mindful
that observability data may concern real people and organizations. Harassment,
discrimination, doxxing, and publishing private telemetry are not tolerated.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Reports
will be reviewed fairly and handled with discretion. Maintainers may remove
content, limit participation, or take other action needed to protect the
community.
